Where did This & Sis come from?

This & Sis sprouted from the dream of a sister who wanted to do something big and amazing with her sisters, who were just as crafty and even more so amazing. Sanya, a crocheter/eco-resiner and the owner of Loop to Loop, wanted to make a storefront that would gather the expertise of all of her sisters in one place for the world to see. She believed that when making this dream store, she would be able to showcase the beauty of different types of handmade items, as well as the talents of her sisters.
