Frequently Asked Questions

If you have any store or item specific questions, we reccomened reaching out to the store specifically thorugh our CONTACT FORM. You will recieve an answer within 24 hours.

How do I place an order?

This & Sis is commited to aiding lovers of handmade items of getting to know the ones that they are getting something made from. To place an order for something on the website, a custom version, or a something you saw or want made, just pop on over to our contact form, fill it out and make sure to select the store that you want to talk to. They will be in contact with you within 24 hours.

What kinds of material do each of the stores use?

Each of the stores are commited to using quality materials but if there is anything you want to ask, specifically, you can use our contact form to recieve that information.

What are the shipping charges?

Shipping fees will depend on the store. Sometimes the stores will have different options of getting your item to you, which would need to be discussed with the store owner.

How do I know that the items are not from Temu or other wholesale platforms?

This & Sis denounces any platform that allows for art to be stolen & wholesale products to be unethically created and sold.All the stores on this website are also commited to this value and would result in removal if found otherwise.

What does "custom" mean?

Custom means that you, an individual with the love for handmade and the arts, get to talk with the creator to make and item that is specific to you. For example, that could be changing the colors of flowers for a crochet item, bead colors for a bracelet, or what is one the acrylic keychain. Sky is the limit and we cannot wait to work with you to create an amazing item!
